IndexGenie and IndexGenie Pro are 100% offline knowledge base and note-taking applications. All user content—including entry titles, notes, checklists, smart templates, ratings, #tags, collections, drafts, wiki links, version history revisions, and local vault PIN hashes—is processed and stored solely in the app's private on-device sandbox on your Android device. The developer does not operate any server and never receives or collects your data. The applications require zero internet permission, have no accounts, no advertisements, and contain no third-party analytics, tracking, or telemetry SDKs.

Paid Pro Edition & Store Purchases: If you purchase IndexGenie Pro as a paid one-time purchase app, transaction and billing processing is handled entirely by the respective app store platform (Huawei AppGallery or Google Play) under their own terms and privacy policies. The app itself never collects, accesses, stores, or transmits payment, card, or banking information.

Exports & System Sharing: If you export an A4 PDF document, Markdown archive (.md), spreadsheet (.csv), or JSON backup file, the app writes the file directly to the local storage destination you pick via the Android Storage Access Framework. If you choose the Share action, the text is passed via the official Android system chooser to your chosen target app. No content is shared or exported without your explicit action.

Private Vault: The optional private vault feature uses a local cryptographic hash (SHA-256 with a random salt) stored directly on your device. It functions completely offline without any network authentication.

Your notes remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app storage, or uninstall the app. For questions or privacy inquiries, contact AppsMakerDeluxe Studios at the address above. These apps are not specifically directed to children.
